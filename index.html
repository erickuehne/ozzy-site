Ozzy by Eric Kuehne

An automated assistant application, operated by Eric Kuehne, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Ozzy is a software application that sends automated reminders and notifications to its enrolled users. It reminds them about school assignments and upcoming tests, keeps a shared calendar and shopping list up to date, and answers questions they send it. Every message is generated and sent by the application itself; nobody types them by hand.

Who receives messages from Ozzy

Only enrolled users, who are members of the operator's household. There is no public sign-up. A household member opts in on the opt-in page, by entering their mobile number and ticking a consent box, or in person, by giving verbal consent (the consent script is in the SMS Terms); the operator then adds the number to the application. Messages are sent by SMS or through the Telegram messaging app, depending on what each user prefers.

What the messages are

  • Reminders the person asked for ("remind me at 7 pm to finish my homework").
  • School heads-ups: an assignment due tomorrow, a test next week.
  • Replies to questions the person sent to Ozzy.
  • Occasional family coordination, such as collecting dinner orders or vacation ideas.

Message frequency varies with use; typically a few messages a day at most. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts at any time, or HELP for help.
