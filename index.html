Ozzy

A private family assistant, operated by Eric Kuehne, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Ozzy is a small piece of software that helps one household stay organized. It reminds family members about school assignments and upcoming tests, keeps the family calendar and shopping list up to date, and helps with everyday questions. It is not a business and is not offered to the public.

Who receives messages from Ozzy

Only members of the household. There is no public sign-up. A family member is added by the account holder, at that person's request and with their consent. Messages are sent by SMS or through the Telegram messaging app, depending on what each person prefers.

What the messages are

  • Reminders the person asked for ("remind me at 7 pm to finish my homework").
  • School heads-ups: an assignment due tomorrow, a test next week.
  • Replies to questions the person sent to Ozzy.
  • Occasional family coordination, such as collecting dinner orders or vacation ideas.

Message frequency varies with use; typically a few messages a day at most. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts at any time, or HELP for help.
