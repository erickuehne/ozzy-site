Privacy Policy

Ozzy, operated by Eric Kuehne. Effective September 24, 2026.

Ozzy is an automated assistant application operated by Eric Kuehne. It sends reminders and notifications to its enrolled users, who are members of the operator's household. This policy explains what information Ozzy handles and how it is treated.

Information Ozzy handles

  • Contact details of household members who ask to use Ozzy: name, mobile phone number and, if they use it, their Telegram username.
  • Messages exchanged with Ozzy, kept so it can carry on a conversation and honor reminders it was asked to send.
  • School information that a household member connects on purpose, such as assignment due dates from their school's learning platform, and family calendar and list entries.

How the information is used

Only to provide the assistant to the household: sending the reminders and replies that members asked for, keeping the family calendar and lists, and reporting school due dates to the family. Ozzy runs on a computer owned by the account holder; information is stored there.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers and text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party, including lead generators. The only outside services involved are those needed to deliver the assistant itself: the messaging carrier used to send and receive SMS, the Telegram messaging service where a member chooses it, and the AI language-model provider that generates replies, each of which receives only what is necessary to perform that function.

Text messaging

Household members receive SMS messages only after asking to be added and consenting. Message frequency varies with use. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out; reply HELP for help. See the SMS Terms and Conditions.

Children

Ozzy is used by a family, and some members are minors. They are added only by their parent, who is the account holder, and their information is handled as described above and never shared for any commercial purpose.

Retention and removal

Information is kept while a member uses Ozzy. A member can ask the account holder to remove their information at any time, and it will be deleted from the assistant.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
