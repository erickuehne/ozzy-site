SMS Terms and Conditions

Ozzy family assistant, operated by Eric Kuehne. Effective September 24, 2026.

Program description

Ozzy sends text messages to members of one household who have asked to use it: reminders they requested, school heads-ups such as assignments due and upcoming tests, replies to questions they sent, and occasional family coordination messages.

Opting in

There is no public sign-up. A household member opts in in person, by asking the account holder to add their mobile number to Ozzy and giving verbal consent. Consent is never requested by text message. Before adding a number, the account holder reads the following and adds the number only after the person answers "yes":

"I'd like to add your mobile number to Ozzy, our family assistant. Ozzy will text you reminders you ask for, school updates such as assignments due and upcoming tests, and occasional family messages such as collecting dinner orders. Message frequency varies, usually a few messages a day at most. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop the texts, or HELP for help. The terms and privacy policy are at erickuehne.github.io/ozzy-site. Do you agree to receive text messages from Ozzy at this number?"

The account holder keeps a record of each member's name, mobile number and the date they consented. Consent is not a condition of anything, and a member can withdraw it at any time.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with use and with what each person asks for; typically a few messages a day at most.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply according to the terms of the member's mobile carrier.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. A confirmation will be sent, after which no further texts will be sent unless the member opts in again by replying START.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact erickuehne@gmail.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

How information is handled is described in the Privacy Policy. Mobile information is never shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
