Opt in to Ozzy text messages

Ozzy by Eric Kuehne, an automated assistant application for one household.

Ozzy is private to the operator's household: only family members can be enrolled, and there is no public sign-up. If you are a member of the household and want Ozzy to text you, fill in this form. Submitting it opens an email to Eric Kuehne, the operator, with your request; he adds your number to the application by hand and you then receive one confirmation text.

Your email app should have opened with the request. If it didn't, email erickuehne@gmail.com with your name and mobile number and say that you agree to receive Ozzy's texts.

What happens next

  • Eric adds your number to Ozzy and records your name, number and the date you consented.
  • You get one confirmation text: "Ozzy by Eric Kuehne: You're enrolled for automated reminders and school updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop the texts, or HELP for help. You can also just tell Eric.

Opting in in person

A household member can also opt in verbally. The operator reads the consent script published in the SMS Terms and adds the number only after the person agrees.
